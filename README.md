## Overview & Three Core Pillars

1. **"What Kind of Music Do I Make?" (`/` & `/artists/*`)**:
   - Curated roster directory of all 7 musical projects (*The Stardust Engine*, *Fractured Prisms*, *The Paper Wall*, *The Arctic Symphony Project*, *Crimson Node*, *Mana Canvas*, and *Firelight*) that hands visitors directly to **The Listening Room** (`https://music.raggiesoft.com`) for lossless/progressive streaming and synchronized lyrics, and **RaggieSoft Book Reader** (`https://books.raggiesoft.com`) for companion novels.

3. **The Corporate Entity of Engine Room Records (`/corporate`)**:
   - Clearly separates the **Real-World Layer** (**Engine Room Records** — strictly no *"LLC"*, the real-world creative brand/imprint under which Michael P. Ragsdale publishes his music) from the **In-Universe Lore Layer** (**Engine Room Records, LLC**, founded by Holly O'Connell in 1992, and the $2.4B conglomerate of **The O'Connell Family Revocable Trust** in `corporate-structure.json`).

## Closed Submission Policy

**Engine Room Records** is a strictly closed private imprint created solely for Michael P. Ragsdale's multimedia projects. Any unsolicited submissions sent to our domains are automatically and permanently deleted before reaching any inbox.
